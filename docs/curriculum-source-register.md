## Content policy

- Official curriculum design pages verify relevant learning-area/subject names and visible Grade 9 PDF filenames. The supplied Grade 6 book excerpt itself identifies Strand 1 / Sub-strand 1.1 and its five steps; official KICD design outcomes have not been independently mapped in this task.
- Before mapping or publishing any outcome, strand, topic, worked example or question, record the specific design title/version, PDF URL, page/section, and access date here.
- Do not infer that Chemistry or Physics is a standalone Grade 6/Grade 9 subject. Their topic groupings remain unavailable until the applicable Science & Technology or Integrated Science design is inspected and cited.
- Do not treat AI-generated or demonstration content as reviewed, approved, or curriculum-verified.
